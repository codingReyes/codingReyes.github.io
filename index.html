About me

PROFILE SUMMARY

I am a computer science graduate and research technician at the Institut de Robòtica i Informàtica Industrial (IRI), CSIC–UPC, working in the Perception and Manipulation research line. My work sits at the intersection of computer vision, deep learning and robotics, with a focus on bringing perception systems out of controlled benchmarks and into challenging real-world environments.

My current research and technical work includes visual recognition for greenhouse cotton monitoring, crop growth-stage detection, leaf segmentation and pest/disease analysis, as well as robotic perception for manipulation tasks involving deformable objects.

I completed my Computer Science studies at the Universitat Politècnica de Catalunya (UPC) (2019–2024), and have since been involved in research projects at IRI.
